history_edu Our Origin Story

Born from the Ancient Ridges of Chandranath & Khaiyachora

Sitakunda is one of Bangladesh's most majestic sanctuaries — where precipitous emerald mountains plunge into the coastal waters of the Bay of Bengal, cradling legendary multi-tier waterfalls, hidden cascades, and sacred shrines.

Yet, for years, adventurous travelers faced steep roadblocks: unmarked slippery routes, unreliable transport bargaining at bus stops, sudden monsoon flash floods, and a critical lack of emergency assistance on remote trails. Many visitors found their dream journey overshadowed by logistical anxiety.

In 2024, a team of native Sitakunda trail leaders, environmentalists, and outdoor veterans united to solve this fundamentally. We created EZ Trails Sitakunda with a singular mission: to make Sitakunda's raw, wild wonder accessible, comfortable, and unequivocally safe for every traveler — from first-time solo backpackers and university groups to families and corporate retreats.

Uncompromising Trail Safety

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about traveling Sitakunda with EZ Trails.

Why do I need a certified guide for Sitakunda waterfalls?

expand_more
Sitakunda's cascades (especially Khaiyachora, Napittachora, and Jhorjhori) involve narrow slippery rock crevices, deep natural plunge pools, and unpredictable water surges during rain. Certified EZ Trails guides know every footing, carry life jackets and first-aid kits, and prevent wrong trail detours.

Is EZ Trails suitable for solo female travelers and families?

expand_more
Yes, 100%. We have tailored family-comfort packages and dedicated protocols for solo female adventurers. Our coordinators are thoroughly vetted, polite, and maintain zero tolerance for harassment, ensuring a safe, respectful, and dignified journey.

Where will the EZ Trails coordinator meet us?

expand_more
Our team receives you directly from your arrival point in Sitakunda — whether it's Sitakunda Bus Station, Sitakunda Railway Station, or the Highway Bypass Stand. We handle your luggage transition to the base station immediately.

How do I book or request a customized itinerary?

expand_more
You can book directly in 2 minutes on our Instant Booking Page, build a custom plan via Trip Planner, or simply message our coordinator on WhatsApp at 01939627110.
